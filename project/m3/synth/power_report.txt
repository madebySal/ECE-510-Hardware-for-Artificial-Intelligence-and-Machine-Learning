BREAKDOWN NOTES
---------------
• Sequential (51.6%) — 1196 DFFs with synchronous enable.
  Dominated by the 128-byte SPI register file (1024 DFFs) which toggles
  partially on every SPI transaction. Internal power of DFFs at 100 MHz is
  significant even when not all FFs toggle.

• Clock (45.5%) — The clock tree driving 1196 DFFs accounts for nearly half
  the total power. This is typical for register-file-heavy designs where the
  clock network must reach every flop.

• Combinational (2.9%) — The XNOR+popcount compute core and SPI decode logic
  together contribute only 264 µW. The compute core itself (74 XNORs + adder
  tree) contributes a small fraction of this — the BNN compute is extremely
  power-efficient.

• Leakage: 46 nW — negligible.
